# Singapore Food Quick Look — privacy policy

**Publisher:** GetLean Clinic. **Last updated:** 26 October 2026.

Singapore Food Quick Look reads only the text you have highlighted on the current tab when you open the extension and press **Use selection**. It uses that text in the popup to match a small food-reference list. You can instead type a food name. If you use the nutrition-label calculator, you enter energy per 100 g and an amount in grams. Those values are used only to show the calculation in the popup.

The extension does not save or transmit highlighted text, food searches, label values, browsing history, health details or identifiers. It does not run in the background, scan pages automatically, use analytics, or create an account. It does not share this information with GetLean Clinic, HPB or any third party. The bundled SG FoodID reference records are stored in the extension package and looked up locally.

The popup contains optional links to SG FoodID, its data licence and a GetLean Clinic food article. If you choose to click one, your browser opens that separate site and its own privacy terms apply. The extension does not attach your highlighted text or label values to those URLs.

The extension uses `activeTab` and `scripting` solely for the user-initiated highlighted-text lookup. It does not request persistent access to every website.

For privacy questions, use the contact route published on [GetLean Clinic](https://www.getleanclinic.sg/contact). If the extension's data practices change, this policy and the relevant store disclosures will be updated before release.
