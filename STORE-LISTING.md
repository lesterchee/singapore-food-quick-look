# Chrome Web Store submission copy — draft for Lester

**Name:** Singapore Food Quick Look

**Short description:** Look up selected Singapore foods or calculate energy from a nutrition label.

**Detailed description:**

Checking a menu? Highlight a food name, open Singapore Food Quick Look and press **Use selection**. Or type the name directly. The extension shows matching SG FoodID reference servings with their energy values, preparation, serving size, data source type and year. It shows alternatives when a term such as “chicken rice” could mean more than one preparation. For foods outside the small reference list, enter energy per 100 g from a nutrition label and the amount in grams to calculate the label-based energy for that amount.

The extension contains ten SG FoodID reference records read on 26 October 2026. Actual recipes, portions and ingredients vary by stall. Figures are general food information, not personal targets or medical advice. SG FoodID data is used under its specific licence with acknowledgement in the extension; HPB does not endorse this tool.

GetLean Clinic is a Singapore doctor-led weight-loss programme designed to end. The popup offers one optional link to a food explainer on the clinic site.

**Single purpose:** A user-initiated food-energy reference and label arithmetic in the browser popup.

**Permission justification:** `activeTab` and `scripting` are used only after the user opens the popup and presses **Use selection**, so the extension can read the highlighted text in the current tab. The typed lookup and label calculator need no website access. No background scanning, host-wide permission, storage or analytics.

**Data handling disclosure:** Selected text, typed searches and nutrition-label entries are processed locally in the popup; none is retained or transmitted. The static food records are bundled. Clicking an optional outbound link opens its destination in a new tab.
