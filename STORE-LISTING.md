**Name:** Singapore Food Quick Look

**Short description:** Look up calories and protein for hawker dishes, kopi and local foods. Highlight a name or type it. Data from HPB's SG FoodID.

**Detailed description:**

Checking a menu or a recipe page? Highlight a dish name, open Singapore Food Quick Look and press Use selection, or just type it. You see calories and protein for a standard serving of 757 Singapore dishes and drinks, including chicken rice, laksa, nasi lemak, bak chor mee, kopi and teh. Search in English, Chinese or the name you use at the stall. Compare similar dishes, such as steamed and roasted chicken rice. Not on the list? Enter the energy per 100 g from a label and the amount, and it does the sum. Figures come from the Health Promotion Board's SG FoodID database (accessed 12 November 2026) and describe a reference serving, not any particular stall. General food information, not a personal target or medical advice. HPB does not endorse this extension. No account, no tracking: your searches never leave your browser. Source code is public on GitHub. Made by GetLean Clinic, a Singapore doctor-led weight-loss programme.

**What's new (0.2.0):** 757 dishes, protein per serving, local names (English, Chinese, Malay).

**Single purpose:** Food energy and protein reference for Singapore dishes.

**Permission justification:** `activeTab` and `scripting` are used only after the user opens the popup and presses **Use selection**, so the extension can read the highlighted text in the current tab. The typed lookup and label calculator need no website access. No background scanning, host-wide permission, storage or analytics.

**Data handling disclosure:** Selected text, typed searches and nutrition-label entries are processed locally in the popup; none is retained or transmitted. The static food records are bundled. Clicking an optional outbound link opens its destination in a new tab.
